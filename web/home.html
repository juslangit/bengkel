How they fit together

  1. 1 boneka makes it — type what you want, watch Blender build it, press once for a skeleton.
  2. 2 gerak moves it — click a joint, turn it, key the pose. Or give a skeleton to something that has none.
  3. 3 Out it goes — .glb for Godot, .fbx for Unreal, .blend to finish by hand, or a rendered video.

Anything either tool makes can be handed straight to the other without going through Finder. Each of them still works perfectly well on its own — sanggar is where they meet, not what they are.
